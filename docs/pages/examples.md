# Examples

Three example repositories are kept in the
[repository](https://github.com/Reflow-AI/aorf/tree/main/examples). The pages linked below are the
output of `aorf build` over them, regenerated by CI on each commit, so they show the current
behaviour of the tool rather than a design mockup.

All three validate under `aorf check --strict` with no errors and no warnings, which is enforced in
CI.

Two things in this example are worth reading in the source rather than the dashboard. The
[NER masking experiment](https://github.com/Reflow-AI/aorf/blob/main/examples/topic-clustering/questions/article-topic-clustering/questions/proper-noun-handling/experiments/001-ner-masking/index.md)
is a refuted hypothesis whose write-up explains why a correct model still produced no gain — the
kind of conclusion that is normally lost. The
[abandoned exploration](https://github.com/Reflow-AI/aorf/blob/main/examples/topic-clustering/questions/topic-drift-detection/experiments/001-bag-of-terms-exploration/index.md)
records a two-day attempt and the reason it was stopped.

## Notes on the data

The example repositories contain no runnable pipeline and no real datasets. The dataset documents
describe corpora that are not committed, since the point is to demonstrate the `storage`,
`generated` and `supersedes` fields rather than to distribute data. The sweep in `runs.jsonl` is
generated so that the counts quoted in `verdict_basis` are true of the file.

Every page here was produced by the same renderer `aorf serve` uses locally, over the same
projection `aorf show --json` prints.
