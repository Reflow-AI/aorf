# Problem statement
72% of trial signups never reach a paid plan. We do not know which step loses them.

# Goal and success criteria
Raise trial-to-paid conversion from 0.28 to 0.35 or better, measured on a full monthly cohort.

# Scope and non-goals
In scope: the signup and onboarding flow. Out of scope: pricing, paid acquisition channels.
