# What is already known
Card-up-front trials convert a smaller share of signups but produce higher-intent users. Form length effects are documented and consistently small.

# What is not settled for us
Whether our activation checklist helps or adds friction. No external source covers our specific flow.

# Conclusion
Partially solved. Do not re-derive the direction of the card effect, but measure its magnitude on our own funnel.
