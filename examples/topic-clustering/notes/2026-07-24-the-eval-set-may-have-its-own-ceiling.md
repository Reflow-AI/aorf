---
title: The eval set may have its own ceiling
brief: >-
  Two editors labelled overlapping slices of the 50-article evaluation set and did not fully
  agree with each other. If inter-editor agreement is below our ARI target, the target is not
  reachable by definition.
date: 2026-07-24
status: open
---

Nobody has measured inter-annotator agreement on the evaluation set. The success criterion in
the [root index](/index.md) is ARI 0.85 against editor labels, which quietly assumes the editor
labels are one consistent thing.

This is not a reason to change the target yet — it is a reason to find out what the ceiling
actually is before spending more runs chasing the last few points.
